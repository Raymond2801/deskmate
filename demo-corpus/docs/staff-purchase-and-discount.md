# Staff Purchase and Discount Policy

**Effective:** 1 July 2025
**Owner:** Retail Operations

---

## 1. Discount rates

| Item type | Discount |
|---|---|
| Full price stock | 30% off |
| Reduced or sale stock | 10% off |
| Gift cards | No discount |
| Clearance items marked "Final Sale" | No discount |

## 2. Who the discount is for

The staff discount is for **your own personal use only**.

It may **not** be used to purchase items for friends, family, or anyone else, and items purchased on staff discount may not be resold.

Misuse of the staff discount is treated as a serious breach of the code of conduct.

## 3. How to process a staff purchase

- Staff purchases must be processed by **another team member**, never by yourself on your own POS login
- A Store Manager or 2IC must be present in store at the time
- Keep your receipt. Staff purchases may be audited.

## 4. Annual limit

Staff purchases at discount are capped at **$2,000 of discounted value per financial year**, per team member.

## 5. Bags and personal items

All bags and personal purchases are kept in your locker during your shift, not on the shop floor or in the stockroom.
