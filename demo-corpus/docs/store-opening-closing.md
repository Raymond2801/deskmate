# Store Opening and Closing Procedures

**Owner:** Retail Operations

---

| Perth CBD | 9am to 5:30pm | 9am to 9pm | 9am to 5:30pm | 9am to 5pm | 11am to 5pm |
| Fremantle | 9am to 5:30pm | 9am to 9pm | 9am to 5:30pm | 9am to 5pm | 11am to 5pm |
| Joondalup | 9am to 5:30pm | 9am to 9pm | 9am to 5:30pm | 9am to 5pm | 11am to 5pm |

## Opening

1. Arrive **30 minutes before trade** commences.
2. Disarm the alarm at the rear entrance. Alarm codes are individual, do not share yours.
3. Turn on lights, POS terminals, and music (staff playlist only).
4. Count the till float. The standard float is **$300** per register, made up per the float breakdown card in the safe.
5. Check the fitting rooms and stockroom are clear and tidy.
6. Set the front display and open the roller door on the hour.
7. Read the previous day's handover note in the diary.

## Closing

1. Announce closing to customers 15 minutes before close.
2. Cash out each register. Count the float back to $300 and bag the balance.
3. Settle the EFTPOS terminals and attach the settlement receipt to the banking sheet.
4. Complete the banking bag and place it in the safe. **Banking is not taken off site by team members.**
5. Tidy the floor, action any go-backs, and vacuum.
6. Write the handover note in the diary.
7. Set the alarm and lock the rear entrance.

## Two-person rule

For any shift finishing **after 6pm**, at least **two team members** must be rostered to close, and must leave the premises together.

Team members should not be alone in the store at any time after trade has ended.

## If the alarm activates

Do not re-enter the store. Call the monitoring company on the number on the alarm panel sticker, then call your Store Manager.
