# Workplace Health and Safety

**Last reviewed:** 20 January 2026
**Owner:** Retail Operations
**Next review:** January 2027

---

## 1. Our commitment

Southline is committed to providing a safe workplace for team members, customers and contractors, consistent with our obligations under Western Australian work health and safety legislation.

## 2. Reporting incidents

All incidents, injuries and near misses must be reported to your Store Manager **within 24 hours** of the event.

The incident report form is in the **incident folder in the back office**, and a copy is emailed to People & Culture once completed.

Near misses are reported the same way as injuries. A near miss that goes unreported is the next injury.

## 3. Manual handling

- The maximum weight for a **single person lift is 20kg**. Anything heavier is a two-person lift or uses the trolley.
- Bend at the knees, keep the load close, and do not twist while carrying.
- Do not lift above shoulder height from the floor in one movement.

## 4. Ladders and step platforms

- Ladders and step platforms are used by **two people**: one on the ladder, one footing it.
- Do not stand on the top two steps.
- Do not use ladders during trading hours in a customer aisle without coning the area off.

## 5. First aid

Each store has a nominated first aid officer and a first aid kit in the back office. The kit is checked monthly by the 2IC.

The current first aid officers are listed on the noticeboard in each back office.

## 6. Emergency evacuation

On hearing the evacuation alarm, direct customers to the nearest exit and proceed to the assembly point. Do not stop to collect belongings and do not use lifts.

Assembly points are marked on the evacuation diagram beside each exit.

## 7. Hazard reporting

Any hazard that cannot be fixed immediately (a spill, a broken fixture, a trip hazard) must be cordoned off and logged in the hazard register in the incident folder.
