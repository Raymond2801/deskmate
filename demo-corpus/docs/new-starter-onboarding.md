# New Starter Onboarding

**Effective:** 1 February 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture

---

## Before your first shift

Your Store Manager will send you:

- Your letter of offer and position description
- Tax file number declaration and superannuation choice form
- Your first two weeks of rostered shifts

## Week 1 checklist

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Store tour, meet the team, uniform and locker issued, POS login created |
| Day 1 | Emergency exits, first aid kit and incident folder walkthrough |
| Day 2 | Buddy shift on the floor, product basics for your section |
| Day 3 | POS training: sales, returns, gift cards, laybys |
| Day 5 | Name badge issued |
| End of Week 1 | Check-in with Store Manager |

## Your first shift buddy

Every new starter is paired with an experienced team member for their first three shifts. Your buddy is your first point of contact for day to day questions on the floor.

## Who to ask

1. **Your buddy** — floor questions, product, where things live
2. **Your Store Manager or 2IC** — policy, roster, pay, anything you are unsure about
3. **Area Manager** — if your Store Manager is unavailable or the matter concerns them

There is no such thing as a question that is too small. If you are unsure about a policy, ask before you act.

## Systems access

You will be set up with:

- A POS operator number (individual, do not share)
- An alarm code if you are rostered to open or close
- Access to the team roster

Systems access is requested by your Store Manager and can take up to five business days.
