# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Version:** 1.4
**Effective:** 15 June 2023
**Owner:** Retail Operations

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Southline team members handle customer returns, refunds and exchanges.

## 2. Change of mind returns

Customers may return an item for **change of mind within 14 days** of purchase, provided:

- The item is unworn and unused
- Original tags are attached
- The customer produces the **original register receipt**. Bank statements and order confirmations are not accepted.

Where these conditions are met, the customer is offered a **store credit only**. Cash and card refunds are not provided for change of mind.

## 3. Items excluded from change of mind returns

- Underwear and swimwear
- Sale items
- Gift cards

## 4. Faulty items

Faulty items are assessed by the Store Manager. Customers have rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## 5. Approval thresholds

| Transaction value | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to $150 | Any team member |
| Over $150 | Store Manager |
| Over $800, or no receipt | Area Manager |

## 6. Processing

Process all returns through the POS return function and record the reason code.
