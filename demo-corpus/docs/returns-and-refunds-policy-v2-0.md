# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective:** 1 September 2025
**Owner:** Retail Operations

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Southline team members handle customer returns, refunds and exchanges across all stores.

## 2. Change of mind returns

Customers may return an item for **change of mind within 30 days** of purchase, provided:

- The item is unworn and unused
- Original tags are attached
- The customer provides **proof of purchase** (receipt, tax invoice, bank statement, or order confirmation email)

Where these conditions are met, the customer may choose a **refund, exchange, or store credit**. Refunds are processed to the original payment method.

## 3. Items excluded from change of mind returns

- Underwear, base layers and swimwear
- Items marked "Final Sale" at the point of purchase
- Gift cards
- Items that have been personalised or altered

## 4. Faulty items

Where an item is faulty, not fit for purpose, or does not match its description, the customer has rights under the **Australian Consumer Law** that are not limited by this policy and have **no fixed time limit**.

Do not refuse a faulty item return on the basis of the 30-day window. Faulty items are assessed case by case, and where the fault is major the customer chooses the remedy.

## 5. Approval thresholds

| Transaction value | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to $200 | Any team member |
| Over $200 | Store Manager or 2IC |
| Over $1,000, or no proof of purchase | Area Manager |

## 6. Processing

All returns are processed through the POS return function against the original transaction where possible. Record the reason code. Returned stock is either returned to floor, sent to the fault bin, or written off, per the condition assessment.
