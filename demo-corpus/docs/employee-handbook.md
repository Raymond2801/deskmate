# Southline Outdoor Co. — Employee Handbook

**Version:** 3.1
**Effective:** 1 March 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture
**Applies to:** All stores (Perth CBD, Fremantle, Joondalup)

---

## 1. Employment types

Southline engages team members on three bases:

- **Full-time** — 38 ordinary hours per week.
- **Part-time** — agreed minimum hours set out in your letter of offer. Any change to your minimum hours must be agreed in writing.
- **Casual** — no guaranteed hours. Casual team members receive a 25% casual loading in place of paid leave entitlements.

## 2. Probation

All new team members serve a probation period of **six months** from their start date. During probation, either party may end the employment with one week's notice.

A probation review is held at the 3-month and 6-month marks with your Store Manager.

## 3. Hours and breaks

Ordinary trading hours vary by store (see the store opening and closing procedures document).

Break entitlements:

| Shift length | Paid rest break | Unpaid meal break |
|---|---|---|
| Under 4 hours | None | None |
| 4 to 5 hours | 1 x 10 minutes | None |
| Over 5 hours | 1 x 10 minutes per 4 hours worked | 1 x 30 minutes |

Meal breaks are **unpaid** and are deducted from your recorded shift time. You must clock out and back in for meal breaks.

## 4. Annual leave

Full-time and part-time team members accrue **four weeks** of paid annual leave per year, accruing progressively and pro rata for part-time hours.

Casual team members do not accrue annual leave.

For how to request leave, see the rostering and leave document.

## 5. Personal (sick and carer's) leave

Full-time and part-time team members accrue **10 days** of paid personal leave per year, pro rata for part-time hours.

A medical certificate or statutory declaration is required when you are absent for **two or more consecutive shifts**, or for any absence on a public holiday or on the day immediately before or after one.

Casual team members may take unpaid personal leave but must still notify the store (see rostering and leave).

## 6. Notice of termination

Notice periods follow the National Employment Standards. As a guide:

- Less than 1 year of service — 1 week
- 1 to 3 years — 2 weeks
- 3 to 5 years — 3 weeks
- More than 5 years — 4 weeks

## 7. Uniform

New team members are issued **two branded shirts** on their first shift, replaced annually or as needed due to fair wear and tear. Trousers, shorts and footwear are your own, and must be closed-toe, in good condition, and in black, navy or charcoal.

Name badges are issued in Week 1 (see new starter onboarding).

## 8. Code of conduct

Team members are expected to:

- Treat customers, colleagues and suppliers with respect.
- Keep commercial and customer information confidential.
- Declare any conflict of interest to their Store Manager in writing.
- Not attend work under the influence of alcohol or illicit substances.

Serious breaches may result in termination without notice.
