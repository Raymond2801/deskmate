# Rostering and Leave Requests

**Effective:** 1 April 2026
**Owner:** Retail Operations

---

## 1. Roster publication

The roster is published **two weeks in advance**, every **Wednesday**, on the team roster and on the back office noticeboard.

Once published, the roster will only change by agreement or where operationally necessary with reasonable notice.

## 2. Availability

Team members submit their availability to their Store Manager in writing. Changes to ongoing availability need **two weeks notice** and are subject to business needs.

## 3. Annual leave requests

- Submit annual leave requests **by email to your Store Manager**
- Minimum **four weeks notice** for any annual leave request
- Requests are approved in the order received, subject to store coverage
- You will receive a written response within one week

**December blackout:** annual leave is not approved between **1 December and 24 December** inclusive, other than in exceptional circumstances approved by the Area Manager.

## 4. Shift swaps

Shift swaps must be **approved by the Store Manager before they take effect**. Team members must not arrange swaps directly between themselves and assume they are approved.

To request a swap, email your Store Manager with both names, the date, and confirmation that the other team member has agreed.

## 5. Calling in sick

If you cannot attend a rostered shift:

- **Call the store** (do not text, do not message a colleague) before **8am**, or at least **two hours before** your shift start if your shift starts before 10am
- Speak to the Store Manager or 2IC on duty
- Advise whether you expect to attend your next shift

See the employee handbook for when a medical certificate is required.

## 6. Public holidays

Stores trade on most public holidays. Public holiday rosters are published four weeks in advance. Penalty rates apply per the relevant award.
